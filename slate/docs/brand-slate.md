# Brand direction — Slate

*July 2026. Frontrunner name. Companion to [naming.md](naming.md).*

## Why Slate

It passes the test that killed the other candidates: the app-becomes-a-verb
reflex. You already say *"put it on the Splitwise" / "did you put that on the
splitwise?"* — Slate slots straight into that grammar because **"on the slate"
is a real, pre-existing idiom** for an unpaid running tab (from the pub
chalk-slate the landlord chalked debts onto). Fettle and Dutch don't fit that
frame; Slate does, while keeping the same warm, textured feel you liked in
Fettle.

- **No bill-splitting competitor owns it** — the category keyword is wide open,
  so you won't be search-invisible the way "Divvy" was.
- **Built-in settle-up metaphor** — when everyone's paid off, you *wipe the
  slate clean*. The zero-balance state names itself.
- **Verbs and nouns cleanly** — "put it on the slate," "I'll slate it," "it's
  all on the slate."

## The decision: wordmark vs domain

**App/brand name: `Slate`. Domain + tagline voice: `on the slate`.**
Keep the short wordmark for the icon and app-store listing; let the full idiom
carry the URL and marketing copy. Best of both — short and verbable, yet
distinctive and more defensible than the bare word.

### Domains, ranked
1. **ontheslate.com** — the URL *is* the phrase; reinforces the verb every time
   it's seen. Most distinctive, likely gettable.
2. **slate.app** — shortest, if free (check — popular word).
3. **getslate.com / tryslate.com** — standard SaaS fallback.
4. **slateup.com** — puns on "settle up."

Grab the winner + both app-store listings the same day.

## Tagline territory
- "Split the bill. It's on the slate."
- "Whose round was it again?"
- "Every trip, on the slate."
- "Put it on the slate." *(primary CTA)*
- "Wipe the slate clean." *(settle-up / zero-balance screen)*

## Visual identity direction
- **Metaphor**: the pub/schoolroom slate + chalk tally marks — but *modern and
  flat*, not skeuomorphic woodgrain.
- **Colour**: a charcoal "slate" (#2b2f36-ish) as the brand mark colour, chalk
  off-white (#f4f1ea) for the glyph. The dark tile + chalk glyph is mode-stable,
  so the icon reads in both light and dark. One warm accent optional for CTAs.
- **Icon**: a slate tile with a tick or a chalk tally (`///|`). The tally doubles
  as "counting up what's owed."
- **Motion moment**: settling up = a chalk-wipe animation clearing the slate.

## The honest caveats (do these before printing anything)
1. **Trademark**: "Slate" is heavily used in *other* software (Slate magazine,
   Technolutions' college CRM, Slate Auto the EV, Slate Digital audio). None in
   personal finance, so class 36 / bill-splitting goods look clearer than the
   crowded class 9. **Spend ~$250 on a trademark-attorney gut-check for class 36
   before committing.**
2. **Domain**: the bare `slate.com` is Slate magazine — gone. Plan on a modified
   domain (see ranked list); confirm availability yourself at a registrar.
3. **Bare-word SEO is hopeless** (magazine + EV own it) — but irrelevant. You'll
   rank on *"Slate split bills" / "Slate app"* + your own domain, and the
   category term is uncontested.

Verdict: usable and a strong fit — not pristine, but the idiom fit is good
enough to be worth the crowding, provided you take a modified domain and get the
class-36 trademark check.
