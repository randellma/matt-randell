Questions before your trip

Is Slate really free?

Yes. Creating groups, adding unlimited expenses, splitting, multi-currency, and settling up are all free. The only paid extra is AI receipt-scan credits — and only one person in the group needs them to scan for everyone.

How do couples split expenses on a group trip?

In Slate, link the two people into a single wallet. They still order and pay individually, but the group only sees one combined balance for the couple, and the app nets out anything they owe each other. It's the cleanest way to handle couples and families in a shared group.

Do my friends need to create an account?

No. A group in Slate is just a link. Whoever holds the link can add expenses as themselves — or on behalf of the friend who never installs anything. Nobody is left out of the ledger.

Is Slate a good Splitwise alternative for travel?

It's built for exactly that. Where Splitwise is optimised for long-running roommate bills, Slate is optimised for getting a trip group started in seconds, handling couples, splitting itemised receipts, and settling up when everyone gets home.

Does it handle different currencies abroad?

Yes. Log an expense in the currency you actually paid, and Slate converts it to the group's currency using the day's exchange rate — which you can override if you got a different rate.
